Things I've built

A couple of small things that run in production on AWS, both defined as infrastructure-as-code:

  • csa-wrangler — a serverless, event-driven meal planner for CSA shares. Forward the weekly share email and SES, S3, Lambda and DynamoDB turn it into a dinner plan and a grocery list. Errors go to a dead-letter queue and raise a CloudWatch alarm rather than failing quietly.
  • calendar-digest — a scheduled Lambda that emails me a 7-day view of my calendar every morning at 5am, with WSDOT highway alerts flagged against events whose locations sit on an affected route.
  • comms-training — a digital security workshop and nine handouts I wrote and have taught from, on passwords, 2FA, secure messaging, backups and travelling safely. MIT licensed, take them.
